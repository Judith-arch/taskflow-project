      How TaskFlow works

      ✏️ Create

      Fill the form with title, deadline, status, assigned person and priority.

      🔍 Filter & Search

      Use status filters and the search bar to find tasks. Sort by date.

      🔄 Track progress

      Change status inline. The progress bar and stats update automatically.

      📦 Archive

      Marking done or cancelled moves the task to Archived automatically.

      ⚠️ Critical tasks

      Tasks due within 5 days or overdue appear in the critical panel.

      📅 Calendar

      Orange dots mark days with pending task deadlines this month.
