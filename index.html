      How TaskFlow works

      ✏️ Create & manage

      Add tasks with title, deadline, status, priority and assignee. Change status inline and delete with confirmation.

      🔍 Filter & search

      Filter by status, search by title and sort by deadline. Archive completed tasks — they count toward your progress.

      📊 Track & stay on top

      Stats, donut chart and progress bar update live. Critical tasks and calendar keep upcoming deadlines visible at a glance.
